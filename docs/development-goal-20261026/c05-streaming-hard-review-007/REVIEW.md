**SR2 — code-inspection finding, low severity, non-blocking (owner awareness).** If a reader thread dies mid-stream (evidence-file write failure on the launcher-owned temp disk, e.g. disk-full/AV lock, or a broken launcher-stdout pipe after the caller closes it early), that stream stops being drained as well as forwarded; the child then blocks on a full pipe and the unbounded `process.wait()` hangs (the KI path's bounded waits don't apply). The old `communicate()` design drained unconditionally and failed loudly at final forward instead. Not reachable in normal operation, not triggerable by any non-mocked probe, and no plan clause is violated by it (the unbounded wait predates), but the trade (rare loud crash → rare silent hang) is real. Advisory hardening: guard the pump body so a persistence/forward failure keeps draining, or bound the wait.

**SR3 — pre-existing edge observation, unchanged by this repair (code-identical in accepted FREEZE006 bytes).** My first P4 attempt accidentally delivered SIGINT during the planning phase: `_planning_config_guarded`'s `except BaseException` (added with the F2 repair) swallows the KeyboardInterrupt and converts it into the planning-failure fallback, which then launches and completes a single normal domain (observed: overall exit 4 from the fallback child) instead of returning 130/stopping dispatch. Identical in the accepted main bytes, so not a regression of this write-set; recorded so the owner/Root can consider distinguishing interrupts from planning failures.
